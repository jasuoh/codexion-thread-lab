# Codexion test cases
#
# Format:   expect | arguments | description
#
#   invalid  rejected: no simulation output, no crash, no hang
#   survive  no burnout, every coder compiles at least the required number of times
#   burnout  a burnout is printed on time, and nothing is printed after it
#   any      burnout or completion, with all observable log rules checked
#   policy   boundary input may be rejected with a diagnostic or simulated correctly
#
# The expected outcomes are reasoned from the subject's rules (see GUIDE.md,
# section 10). They are not official. Work them out on paper yourself, and add
# your own cases.
